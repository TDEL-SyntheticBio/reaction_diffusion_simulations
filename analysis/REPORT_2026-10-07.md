# JAPI engine and pipeline check, 7 October 2026

Everything here was computed with the unmodified engine at commit `07a328b` through the wrappers in
`analysis/`, with explicit seeds, and re-verified by independent review passes (twelve on the engine
conventions, three on the analysis code). Engine conventions are in `CLAUDE.md`; this file holds the
results. All runs are 100×100 unless stated, dt = 0.01, frames every t = 10. "Synchronous" is the
Supp Table 1 Fig 1C protocol (random_uniform_over0, spike 1.0, no nucleation); "nucleation" is the
Fig 2L/3 protocol (all_off, spike 2.0, nucleation 0.01, kick 4.0).

## 1. What was checked first, and why

The handoff's spatial statistics came from throwaway scripts that no longer exist, its component
counter in the deposit uses the mirror of the engine's adjacency, and every spatial statement was quoted
in units of an inhibitor range that had never been measured on the lattice. So before any screen the
measurement pipeline was rebuilt on the engine's own neighbour arrays and run against known answers,
and the inhibitor range was measured directly on arrested domains.

## 7. What this changes in the handoff and the session notes

1. λ_i for every 2D statement is √(1.5·D_i/γ): 5.5 cells at D_i = 10, 7.7 at D_i = 20.
2. "The hole sits at 2λ_i" was a coincidence with the understated λ. Measured holes: 8.9, 9.7, 11.0,
   16.3 (D_i = 10) and 9.1, 12.2 (D_i = 20) against 2λ = 11.0 and 15.5.
3. "Irregularity comes from asynchronous initiation" rested on counting transient kicks as spots. Under
   the published nucleation protocol the domains are ordered and frozen from t ≈ 50.
4. "Each domain is a deterministic object with a parameter-set width" holds for b_i ≥ 11 on the Fig 1C
   line, not for the regular-spots panel, whose spots self-replicate.
5. The handoff's hex classifier label "pure arrest" for b_i 4–12 covers two mechanisms.
6. The Fig 1C irregular-spots regime is reachable under continuous nucleation (55 small domains), but
   only visible with a kick-independent threshold.
7. Component counts in the deposit's `npz_feature_distribution.py` used mirrored adjacency; thin
   structures fragment (1 → 16 components on a diagonal line).

### Nucleation, stopping criterion and frames

- Kick rate on 100×100 at rate 0.01: 0.94 kicks per step while most cells are off; 0.04 on the
  20×20 Fig 3L grid; 0.56 in the labyrinth run (44% of cells above a_ss). A single-cell kick
  decays below 0.3·r_a within ~0.5 time units.
- Under nucleation the window diff per cell is ≥ 0.05 (median 0.13 on 100×100, 0.10 on 20×20), so
  the 1e-4 stopping criterion never fires. Without nucleation (Fig 1C protocol, 50,000 max): all-on

  spots (b_i = 3, 5, 12) ran the full 50,000 with final window diffs 1.7e-3, 1.2e-3, 4.5e-4.

### (a) Does the lone-seed fate survive crowding?

- **b_i = 5 (lone seed: ring, fragmentation, spreading colony).** Yes, and the colonies do fill the
  field when packed: 9, 36 and 144 seeds all end at coverage 0.242–0.247, the same as the random
  protocol (0.243–0.247). The dense seedings get there by t = 110–250; the 9-seed fields only at
  t = 330–410 (0.216–0.240 at t = 300). They do not jam into a sparse arrested pattern. What the seed
  count changes is the texture, not the amount: from few seeds the field keeps labyrinthine remnants
  of the expanding rings (largest blobs 150–250 cells against 41 for the random start) and ends with
  fewer, larger domains (91–104 blobs of ~50 cells against 167 of 29), and it has not stopped
  rearranging at t = 600: the blob count last changed between t = 450 and 590 in eight of the nine
  seeded runs (steps of up to 6 per frame before t = 450, 0–2 after 500) and cells ON drift by
  10–70 per 100 t. The single colony itself reaches 0.232 by t = 600 and is still growing at about
  7 cells per time unit.
- **b_i = 9 (lone seed: 37-cell disc, static to t ≈ 300, then divides).** Partly. Dense fields
  essentially do not divide: with 144 seeds the blob count is fixed from t = 40 in two replicates and
  from t = 240 in the third, where the one domain that had reached 35 cells (the isolated disc's
  size) split in two; the random start fixes 130–137 blobs by t = 210–390 with decreases only.
  Coverage saturates at 0.10–0.11 and domains are 16–21 cells, well below the isolated 37, which is
  why they do not divide: a crowded domain divides when it reaches ~35 cells, and crowding keeps all
  but one of ~300 domains below that. Sparse fields do what the lone seed does,
  but much earlier: with 9 seeds dumbbells appear before t = 150 and the count rises 9 → 17 → 33 → 58
  by t = 600, coverage 0.077 and still rising; with 36 seeds 33 → 70, coverage 0.09, slowing. The lone
  seed's division is a linear instability of the disc. Its convergence diagnostic first falls to
  2e-10 at t = 100 (the disc is a numerical fixed point) and then grows exponentially at
  σ = 0.071 per time unit (doubling time 10) until the split at t ≈ 350. Started from round-off that
  takes 300 time units; started from a neighbour's halo it takes under 100. The lone-seed division
  time is therefore a property of the perturbation level, not of the parameters, and crowding
  suppresses the instability altogether above roughly 100 domains per 141×141.
- **b_i = 12 (lone seed: 19-cell arrested domain at a fixed point).** Yes, as far as the unit goes.
  Every seed becomes one arrested domain, nothing replicates (no seeded or random run ever gains a
  blob), and the blob count equals the number of seeds that did not merge or fall inside a
  neighbour's halo: 9 → 8–9, 36 → 32–33, 144 → 90–96. Blob counts are fixed by t = 10–40 (one
  144-seed replicate loses a blob at t = 200). Domain size, however, is not the isolated 19: it is
  22–25 cells when sparse (9 seeds, random; domains 3 halo lengths from any neighbour), 20–21 at 36
  seeds, 14 at 144 seeds and 12 under nucleation, a factor of two across protocols at identical
  parameters, and it is not explained by the lone seed's inhibitor preload (a single cell at level 5
  with zero inhibitor also gives 19). Field domains are also not static: nearest-centroid tracking
  across the t = 60/150/300/600 snapshots shows them translating by a median 3–5 cells over 540 time
  units (maxima 5–11), at roughly one cell per 100 t, directed away from the nearest neighbour and
  from the boundaries, while total activator rises monotonically and individual domains wander by a
  few cells in size. The lone seed at the symmetric lattice centre is pinned (total activator
  constant to 2 dp). This drift is what keeps the fields' convergence diagnostic at 1e-4–1e-3
  instead of 1e-16 and their cells ON moving by a few cells per frame.

The published convergence criterion (change per cell-field below 1e-4 over a 200-step window,
after 1,000 steps; `CLAUDE.md` §8) was evaluated on every window of every run. It never fires for any
field run. The synchronous fields' per-run minima lie between 1.3e-4 and 2e-3 (final windows 1.3e-4 to
4.6e-3; the sparse b_i = 12 fields spend most of the run within 1.3–1.6× of the threshold, the b_i = 5
and b_i = 9 9-seed fields above 2e-3) and the nucleation runs sit at 5e-2 to 8e-2, so the published
50,000-step cap, not convergence, is what would have ended them, at t = 500, while the b_i = 5 seeded
fields and the b_i = 9 sparse fields were still changing. It does fire for the two lone seeds: b_i = 12 at
t = 16 (correct, a fixed point) and **b_i = 9 at t = 22, about 335 time units before the domain
divides** (with the batch runner's 10,000-step default it would fire at t = 102, still 255 early). A
convergence stop on a lone seed in this regime certifies a transient.

### (c) Do the two field protocols land in the same place?

- At b_i = 5 coverage is the quantity least dependent on protocol, but not invariant: every
  synchronous condition ends at 0.242–0.247, while continuous nucleation ends systematically higher
  at 0.258–0.259 (0.253 without the transients, about five replicate spreads above). Texture differs
  more: 167 blobs of 29 cells (random) against 140 real domains of 36 (nucleation), and the
  nucleation field is never static (two kicks per step). Neither matches the seeded runs' counts.
- At b_i = 9 the dense conditions are close but resolvable: 144 seeds 103 blobs at coverage 0.105,
  random 133 at 0.110, nucleation 130 real domains at 0.122 (0.114 without transients); the sparse
  seeded fields are below all of these and still rising at t = 600.
- At b_i = 12 nothing is shared but the unit: the random start nucleates 28–31 domains (coverage
  0.03), continuous nucleation 114 (0.08), and seeded fields their surviving seed count. In the arrest
  regime the field is the list of successful nucleation events; the protocol sets how long that list
  is, and crowding sets the size of each entry (23 cells sparse, 12 under nucleation).

In short: the lone-seed fate predicts the field's mechanism class, not its numbers. In the
replicating regime coverage is nearly protocol-independent (0.242–0.247 synchronous, 0.259 under
nucleation) and reached from any seed count, while domain count and morphology stay
history-dependent and unsettled at t = 600; in the arrest regime nothing replicates and the field is
its surviving seeds, but the domain size is set by crowding, not by the parameters alone. At the
boundary (b_i = 9) crowding decides which of the two the field behaves like. In no field run does
the lone seed's timescale carry over: isolated domains are pinned and either fixed or divide on a
round-off-seeded clock, field domains drift and interact, so field settling times are longer than
600 for every condition except the arrested blob counts.

### (a) Does the lone-seed fate survive crowding?

- **Arrest sets (2L_JAPI, 3L, 3B_small, 3H_lowDi, S8D_lowri): yes, for the unit.** No seeded or random
  run gains a blob after t = 60; the only count changes are merges and seeds lost inside a neighbour's
  halo (9 → 8–9, 36 → 28–32, 144 → 60–88, the largest loss at S8D_lowri, b_i = 50). Domain size is
  the lone-seed size only when sparse and shrinks with crowding: 2L_JAPI 91 → 70 → 50 → 27 → 19
  (lone, 9, 36, 144 seeds, random), 3L 127 → 120 → 75 → 35 → 30, 3B_small 61 → 47 → 37 → 19 → 19, and
  the 19-cell units of 3H_lowDi and S8D_lowri drop to 7–8 cells at 144 seeds. The lone size is an
  upper bound: no domain exceeds it except 1–2 of the 9-seed domains at 3L in two replicates, which
  elongate slowly (up to 166 cells against 127; cells ON still rising by 8–36 per 100 t). In the dense
  limit (144 seeds, random, nucleation) coverage converges per set to within replicate spread
  (2L_JAPI 0.093–0.112, 3L 0.149–0.163, 3B_small 0.067–0.088), so the dense-field coverage is a
  property of the parameters reached from any dense start, while sparse starts stay sparse (9 seeds:
  0.006–0.05) because nothing replicates.
- **3B_big (b_a 5, b_i 15): the lone disc is a fixed point the field does not reach.** Its 271-cell
  disc is static to 1e-19 for 900 time units, yet with 9 seeds 62–88% of the domains exceed it
  (median 413–477 cells), elongate into worms and branch; cells ON rise by 60–247 per 100 t at
  t = 500–600 and coverage 0.18–0.20 is still rising about 0.01 per 100 t. With 36 seeds the median is
  111–154 cells and growth has slowed to +7–13 per 100 t; at 144 seeds the domains (48–51 cells) are
  fixed. Random gives 34–36 domains of median 56–90 with worms to 760 cells (coverage 0.27);
  nucleation gives 31–39 real domains of mean 131–161 at coverage rising from 0.21–0.23 (t = 60) to
  0.25–0.26 (t = 600). The symmetric lone disc arrests; a disc with neighbours or a border (19–22 of
  the 34–36 random domains touch the border) elongates. Unlike Fig 1C b_i = 9, where the symmetric disc is linearly unstable and
  neighbours only shorten the wait, here the finished disc is linearly stable (classifier above), so
  a lone-domain test, by fixed point or by perturbation, calls 3B_big "arrest"; only a field test
  shows the worms.
- **3B_labyrinth: the lone seed's class is the field's class, its coverage is not.** Seeded fields end
  at 0.46–0.51, random and nucleation at 0.57–0.58. The random start gives a single connected network
  with holes (one blob of 11,311–11,545 cells), nucleation a labyrinth in 23–26 pieces. In the seeded
  fields hexagonal discs and worms coexist to t = 600: many 36- and 144-seed domains remain compact
  hexagons while others elongate, coverage keeps rising by 1–41 cells per 100 t, and the counts are
  fixed from t = 10–30 except one 144-seed replicate that merges until t = 470.
- **2L_JA**: every condition is uniform ON by t = 12–72.

### (b) Settling time

- **Lone seeds settle by t = 10–50** (cells constant) and are at machine-precision fixed points by
  t = 74–152 in the six arrest sets; 3B_labyrinth and 2L_JA do not arrest.
- **Field blob counts fix early in the synchronous conditions:** t = 10–20 for 9 seeds, 10–90 for 36,
  20–250 for 144 seeds (merges), 10–540 for random (merges continue in a few runs: 2L_JAPI replicate 2
  at t = 500, 3L replicate 3 at 540, 3B_small replicate 3 at 430), never under nucleation (transients).
  Cells ON settle more slowly: 45 of the 105 patterned field runs have cells ON and blobs unchanged
  from some frame to t = 600, 23 of them from t ≤ 500. Thirteen synchronous runs reach a numerical fixed
  point (per-cell change < 1e-12: 2L_JAPI 9 seeds ×3, 36 seeds ×1, random ×1; 3B_small 9 seeds ×3; 3L
  144 seeds ×1; S8D_lowri 9 seeds ×1, random ×3), which no field run did on the Fig 1C line (§10); the
  rest sit between 1e-11 and 8e-4, adjusting by a few cells per 100 t. The 3H_lowDi and S8D_lowri 36-
  and 144-seed fields and the 3H_lowDi random fields stay at 1–6e-4 for the whole run.
- **The 1e-4 criterion fires in 70 of the 120 field runs**: all 15 of 2L_JA (t = 12–72) and 55 of the
  105 patterned runs (14 of 21 with 9 seeds, 11 with 36, 14 with 144, 16 random, none of the 21
  nucleation runs, whose per-cell change is 3.5e-2 to 1.4e-1), at t = 38–594 (median 226). When it
  fires it is safe on these sets: the later change is at most one blob (a merge) and at most 1% of
  cells ON in 64 of the 70, the largest exceptions being +36 cells (3.6%) at 3L 9 seeds replicate 1
  (stop t = 314) and +7 cells (5.8% of 120) at 3H_lowDi 9 seeds replicate 3 (stop t = 74). Where it
  does not fire by t = 600 the field is either still growing (3B_big 9 and 36 seeds and two random
  replicates; 3B_labyrinth 9 seeds) or drifting at 1–6e-4 (3H_lowDi 36/144/random, S8D_lowri 36/144,
  one run each at 2L_JAPI 36 seeds, 3L 9 and 36 seeds). The published 50,000-step cap would have ended
  those at t = 500, with the 3B_big 9-seed coverage (0.17–0.20) still rising.
- **Nucleation fields never settle**: real domain counts are still rising at S8D_lowri (20–25 at
  t = 60 → 28–29 at 600) and 3H_lowDi (14–20 → 43–49), by 1–4 elsewhere, and coverage rises by
  0.004–0.03 over t = 60–600 in every set.

### (c) Do the two field protocols land in the same place?

- **Coverage agrees within replicate spread** at 2L_JAPI (random 0.108–0.110; nucleation 0.093–0.104
  real, 0.103–0.112 total), 3L, 3B_small and 3B_labyrinth, and disagrees at 3B_big (0.27 against
  0.25–0.26 and rising), 3H_lowDi (0.024 against 0.036–0.039 real) and S8D_lowri (0.003–0.006 against
  0.026–0.028).
- **Texture never agrees.** The random start nucleates its domains at once (2L_JAPI: 132–139 blobs at
  t = 10, merging to 94–98 by t = 150) and crowding sets their size (median 19 cells); under nucleation
  the first domains grow to near the lone size before the field is crowded, so at the same coverage
  there are a third as many domains, three times larger (2L_JAPI 29–37 real domains of median 62–73
  against the lone 91; 3L 80–94 against 127; 3B_small 45–52 against 61). On the Fig 1C line (§10) the
  same comparison gave 167 against 140 domains; at n 10/4 it is a factor of three.
- **Small-unit sets (3H_lowDi, S8D_lowri; 19-cell domains): the random start barely nucleates.** U(0, 2)
  makes 3–6 domains at S8D_lowri (b_i = 50) and 34–37 at 3H_lowDi, against 28–29 and 43–49 real
  domains under nucleation at t = 600, still accruing at 1–2 per 100 t. Here the field is the list of
  successful nucleation events, the protocol sets its length, and under nucleation the density has
  not reached a stationary value by t = 600.

In short: on the experiment-matched sets the lone seed predicts the field's class in all eight
(arrest in six, labyrinth, uniform ON), and in the arrest sets its size is the upper bound of the
field's domain size, while the dense-field coverage is set by the parameters (random, nucleation and
144 seeds agree to within ~10% in the three large-domain sets) and the domain count and size at that
coverage are set by the protocol, by a factor of three. The one place the lone seed misleads is
3B_big, one set from the labyrinth, where the symmetric disc is a fixed point but any domain with
neighbours elongates. Unlike the Fig 1C line, the published convergence criterion is safe here: lone
seeds are true fixed points by t ≈ 100, and every one of the 55 patterned field runs it stops changes
by at most 6% of cells ON afterwards; it never stops a nucleation run.
